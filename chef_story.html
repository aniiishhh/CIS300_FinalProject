Meet Chef Srikari

A Passion for Cooking that Began at Home

From a 12-Year-Old Dreamer to a Culinary Expert

Chef Srikari's love affair with the kitchen started when she was just 12 years old. Under the watchful eyes of her parents and grandmother, she learned the delicate art of balancing spices and flavors. But her curiosity didn't stop at family recipes; she spent countless hours watching cooking tutorials on YouTube, absorbing techniques from around the world.

By the age of 14, Srikari took on a challenge that would define her future: she cooked an entire traditional meal for 50 guests at her own birthday party. The overwhelming praise she received that day planted the seed for what would eventually become HomeFlavors.

After moving to the US, she carried these cherished memories and skills with her. HomeFlavors is her way of sharing that same warmth and authentic taste with her new community.

"Cooking is my language of love. I want every dish to feel like a warm hug from home, just like my grandmother's cooking did for me."
